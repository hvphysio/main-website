Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Fracture Recovery?

Post fracture rehabilitation is essential after fracture healing or surgery to restore joint mobility, regain muscle strength, reduce stiffness and safely return to walking and daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps prevent weakness, balance problems and long-term movement limitations.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured fracture rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, strengthening and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Fracture Recovery

  • Joint stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Weakness in surrounding muscles
  • Difficulty walking or weight bearing
  • Pain during movement
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Reduced endurance and flexibility
  • Fear of movement or falling
  • Difficulty returning to daily activities
