Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Shoulder Arthroscopy?

Shoulder arthroscopy rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore shoulder mobility, improve strength, reduce stiffness and safely return to daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps improve arm function and prevent long-term shoulder movement limitations.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on shoulder recovery, mobility improvement and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Shoulder Arthroscopy

  • Shoulder pain and stiffness
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Difficulty lifting the arm
  • Weakness in shoulder muscles
  • Difficulty performing overhead activities
  • Reduced functional arm use
  • Postural imbalance and muscle tightness
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
