Why Does Stiffness Occur After Cast Removal?

Joint stiffness commonly develops after prolonged immobilization in a cast due to reduced movement, muscle weakness and soft tissue tightness. Patients may experience difficulty moving the joint normally after cast removal.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured physiotherapy programs focused on restoring mobility, flexibility, strength and functional movement after cast removal.

Common Problems After Cast Removal

  • Joint stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Muscle weakness after immobilization
  • Pain during movement
  • Difficulty walking or using the limb
  • Reduced flexibility and coordination
  • Balance and mobility problems
  • Swelling and soft tissue tightness
  • Difficulty returning to daily activities
