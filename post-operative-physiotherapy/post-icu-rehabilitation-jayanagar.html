Why Is Physiotherapy Important After ICU Stay?

Patients recovering after ICU stay commonly experience severe muscle weakness, reduced endurance, balance problems and difficulty performing daily activities. Physiotherapy is essential to improve mobility, muscle strength and functional independence safely.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, strengthening, respiratory rehabilitation and functional recovery after critical illness.
