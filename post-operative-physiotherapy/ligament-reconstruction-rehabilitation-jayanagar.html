Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Ligament Reconstruction Surgery?

Ligament reconstruction rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore joint stability, improve mobility, regain muscle strength and safely return to walking, sports and daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps improve movement control and reduce the risk of re injury.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation programs focused on stability recovery, strengthening and functional movement rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Ligament Reconstruction Surgery

  • Joint pain and stiffness
  • Reduced mobility and flexibility
  • Weakness in surrounding muscles
  • Reduced joint stability
  • Difficulty walking or weight bearing
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Movement fear and reduced confidence
  • Difficulty returning to activities
