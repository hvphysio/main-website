Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Hip Labral Repair Surgery?

Hip labral repair rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore hip mobility, improve joint stability, reduce stiffness and safely return to walking and daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps improve movement control and reduce long-term mobility limitations.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on hip mobility recovery, strengthening and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Hip Labral Repair Surgery

  • Hip pain and stiffness
  • Reduced hip mobility
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Weakness in hip and leg muscles
  • Reduced hip stability
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Difficulty returning to activities
