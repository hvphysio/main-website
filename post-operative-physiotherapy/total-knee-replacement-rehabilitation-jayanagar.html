Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Total Knee Replacement?

Total knee replacement rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore knee movement, reduce stiffness, improve walking ability and regain strength safely. Without proper physiotherapy, patients may continue to experience limited knee bending, weakness, swelling and difficulty walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy for total knee replacement patients, focused on safe recovery, mobility improvement and long-term functional independence.

Common Problems After Total Knee Replacement

  • Knee pain and swelling after surgery
  • Reduced knee bending and stiffness
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Weakness in thigh and leg muscles
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Reduced confidence while walking
  • Difficulty returning to daily activities

Post Operative Physiotherapy Treatment

  • Detailed post surgical physiotherapy assessment
  • Knee range of motion exercises
  • Quadriceps and hamstring strengthening
  • Gait training and walking correction
  • Balance and coordination training
  • Stair climbing practice when suitable
  • Functional movement rehabilitation
  • Swelling and stiffness management guidance
  • Progressive mobility training
  • Home exercise rehabilitation program

Advanced Techniques Used at Our Clinic

  • KIASTM for soft tissue restriction
  • DIASTM for myofascial tightness
  • Functional strengthening exercises
  • Gait rehabilitation and movement correction
  • Balance retraining exercises
