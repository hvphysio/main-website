Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Frozen Shoulder Release Surgery?

Frozen shoulder release rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore shoulder mobility, reduce stiffness, improve arm movement and regain functional strength safely. Proper physiotherapy helps prevent recurrence of stiffness and improves long-term shoulder function.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on shoulder mobility recovery, strengthening and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Frozen Shoulder Release Surgery

  • Shoulder pain and stiffness
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Difficulty lifting the arm
  • Weakness in shoulder muscles
  • Difficulty performing overhead activities
  • Reduced functional arm use
  • Muscle tightness and postural imbalance
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
