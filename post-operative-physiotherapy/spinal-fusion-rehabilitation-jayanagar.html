Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Spinal Fusion Surgery?

Spinal fusion rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore mobility, improve posture, reduce pain and safely regain strength and walking ability. Proper physiotherapy helps improve spinal stability and long-term functional recovery after surgery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on spinal recovery, strengthening and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Spinal Fusion Surgery

  • Back or neck pain after surgery
  • Reduced spinal mobility and stiffness
  • Weakness in core and back muscles
  • Difficulty walking or standing for long periods
  • Postural imbalance and muscle tightness
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty returning to daily activities
