Why Is Physiotherapy Important After ACL Reconstruction?

ACL reconstruction rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore knee stability, improve range of motion, regain muscle strength and safely return to walking, sports and daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps reduce stiffness, weakness and movement limitations.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured ACL rehabilitation programs focused on knee stability, strength, balance and long-term recovery.

Common Problems After ACL Reconstruction Surgery

  • Knee pain and swelling
  • Reduced knee mobility and stiffness
  • Weakness in quadriceps and hamstrings
  • Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
  • Reduced knee stability
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Fear of movement or re injury
  • Difficulty returning to sports activities

Post Operative Physiotherapy Treatment

  • Gait training and walking correction
