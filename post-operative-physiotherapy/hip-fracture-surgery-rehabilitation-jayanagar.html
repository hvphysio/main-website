Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Hip Fracture Surgery?

Hip fracture rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore walking ability, improve hip mobility, regain strength and safely return to daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps reduce weakness, stiffness and balance problems after prolonged immobilization or surgery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on mobility recovery, gait rehabilitation and functional independence.

Common Problems After Hip Fracture Surgery

  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Hip pain and stiffness
  • Weakness in hip and leg muscles
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Fear of falling during walking
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
