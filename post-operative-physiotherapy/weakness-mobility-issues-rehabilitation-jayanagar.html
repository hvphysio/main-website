Why Is Physiotherapy Important for Weakness and Mobility Problems?

Muscle weakness and mobility problems can occur after surgery, hospitalization, neurological conditions, prolonged bed rest or injury. Physiotherapy is essential to improve strength, mobility, balance and functional independence safely.
