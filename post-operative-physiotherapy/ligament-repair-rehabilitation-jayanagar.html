Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Ligament Repair Surgery?

Ligament repair rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore joint stability, improve mobility, reduce stiffness and regain muscle strength safely. Proper physiotherapy helps patients return to walking, sports and daily activities while reducing the risk of re injury.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured ligament rehabilitation programs focused on movement correction, strengthening, balance and long-term functional recovery.

Common Problems After Ligament Repair Surgery

  • Joint pain and swelling
  • Reduced joint mobility and stiffness
  • Weakness in surrounding muscles
  • Difficulty walking or weight bearing
  • Reduced joint stability
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Fear of movement or re injury
  • Difficulty returning to activities
