Why Is Physiotherapy Important for Coma Patients?

Physiotherapy is essential for coma patients to help prevent complications caused by prolonged immobility such as joint stiffness, muscle tightness, pressure sores and respiratory complications. Early rehabilitation also supports neurological recovery and functional improvement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured neuro rehabilitation programs focused on positioning, respiratory care, mobility improvement and functional recovery for critically ill neurological patients.

Common Problems in Coma Patients

  • Muscle weakness and stiffness
  • Joint tightness and contractures
  • Reduced mobility and endurance
  • Respiratory complications
  • Postural instability
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty performing functional activities
  • Functional dependency
