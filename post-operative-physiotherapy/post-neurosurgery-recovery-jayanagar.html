Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Neurosurgery?

Post neurosurgery physiotherapy is essential to improve mobility, balance, coordination, muscle strength and functional independence after brain or spinal surgery. Physiotherapy helps patients recover safely and improve movement confidence during rehabilitation.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured neuro rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, balance training and functional rehabilitation after neurosurgery.

Common Problems After Neurosurgery

  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Postural instability
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Movement fear and reduced confidence
  • Functional mobility limitations
