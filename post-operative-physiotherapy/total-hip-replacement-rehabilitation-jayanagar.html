Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Total Hip Replacement?

Total hip replacement rehabilitation is essential after surgery to restore walking ability, improve hip mobility, regain muscle strength and safely return to daily activities. Proper physiotherapy helps reduce stiffness, weakness and movement limitations after surgery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured post operative physiotherapy programs focused on safe walking recovery, balance training and long-term mobility improvement.

Common Problems After Total Hip Replacement Surgery

  • Hip pain and stiffness
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Reduced hip mobility
  • Weakness in hip and leg muscles
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced confidence during walking
  • Difficulty returning to daily activities
