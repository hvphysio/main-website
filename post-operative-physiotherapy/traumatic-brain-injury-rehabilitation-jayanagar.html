Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Traumatic Brain Injury?

Traumatic brain injury rehabilitation is essential to improve mobility, balance, muscle strength, coordination and functional independence after brain injury. Physiotherapy helps patients recover movement abilities safely and improve overall quality of life.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured neurological rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, balance training and functional rehabilitation after traumatic brain injury.

Common Problems After Traumatic Brain Injury

  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Postural instability
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Movement fear and reduced confidence
  • Functional mobility limitations
