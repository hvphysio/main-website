Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Craniotomy Surgery?

Craniotomy rehabilitation is essential after brain surgery to improve mobility, balance, muscle strength, coordination and functional independence. Physiotherapy helps patients recover safely and improve confidence during movement and daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured neurological rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, balance training and functional rehabilitation after neurosurgery.

Common Problems After Craniotomy Surgery

  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Postural instability
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Movement fear and reduced confidence
  • Functional mobility limitations
