Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Hospitalization?

Patients recovering after hospitalization often experience muscle weakness, reduced endurance, balance problems and difficulty performing daily activities. Physiotherapy is essential to improve mobility, regain strength and restore functional independence safely.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation programs focused on mobility recovery, strengthening, balance training and functional improvement after hospital discharge.

Common Problems After Hospital Stay
