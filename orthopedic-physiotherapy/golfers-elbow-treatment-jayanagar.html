What Is Golfer’s Elbow?

Golfer’s elbow, also called medial epicondylitis, is a painful condition affecting the inner side of the elbow. It commonly develops due to repetitive gripping, lifting, wrist bending or sports-related strain involving the forearm muscles.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for golfer’s elbow focused on pain reduction, tendon recovery, grip strength improvement and functional rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Golfer’s Elbow

  • Repetitive gripping or lifting activities
  • Overuse of forearm muscles
  • Sports activities such as golf, badminton or gym workouts
  • Typing and repetitive desk work
  • Poor lifting mechanics
  • Forearm muscle tightness
  • Work-related repetitive strain
  • Weak wrist and elbow stabilizing muscles

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Pain on the inner side of the elbow
  • Forearm pain and tightness
  • Pain while gripping or lifting objects
  • Weak grip strength
  • Pain during wrist movement
  • Difficulty opening jars or carrying objects
  • Pain during sports or repetitive activities
