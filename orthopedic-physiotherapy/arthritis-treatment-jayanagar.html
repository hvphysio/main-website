What Is Arthritis?

Arthritis is a joint condition that causes pain, stiffness, swelling and reduced movement. It commonly affects the knee, hip, shoulder, hand, spine and other joints. In many patients, arthritis gradually affects walking, stair climbing, sitting, standing and daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide arthritis physiotherapy focused on pain relief, joint mobility, muscle strengthening, balance improvement and long-term functional independence.

Common Causes of Arthritis Pain

  • Age-related joint wear and tear
  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Hip arthritis
  • Spine arthritis and spondylosis
  • Previous joint injury
  • Muscle weakness around the joint
  • Obesity and increased joint load
  • Poor posture or altered walking pattern
  • Reduced flexibility and joint stiffness

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Joint pain during walking or movement
  • Morning stiffness or stiffness after rest
  • Swelling around the joint
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Difficulty sitting, standing or squatting
  • Reduced joint movement
  • Weakness around the affected joint
  • Pain that keeps recurring during daily activity
