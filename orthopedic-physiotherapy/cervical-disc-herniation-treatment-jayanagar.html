What Is Cervical Disc Herniation?

Cervical disc herniation occurs when a cervical spinal disc bulges or protrudes and irritates nearby nerves. It commonly causes neck pain, arm pain, tingling, numbness, muscle weakness and reduced neck movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for cervical disc herniation focused on pain relief, nerve decompression, posture correction and long-term cervical rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Cervical Disc Herniation

  • Poor posture and forward neck posture
  • Degenerative cervical disc changes
  • Prolonged computer or mobile use
  • Cervical muscle tightness
  • Improper lifting mechanics
  • Sudden neck injury or trauma
  • Reduced cervical stability
  • Cervical spondylosis

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Neck pain radiating to the arm
  • Tingling or numbness in the fingers
  • Shoulder pain and stiffness
  • Weakness in the arm or hand
  • Pain while turning the neck
  • Reduced neck mobility
  • Headache associated with neck pain
  • Difficulty sitting for long duration
