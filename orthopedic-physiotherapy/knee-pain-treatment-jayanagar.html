Why Knee Pain Should Not Be Ignored

Knee pain can affect walking, stair climbing, squatting, sports activity and daily movement. Many patients ignore knee pain initially, but untreated knee problems may gradually lead to stiffness, weakness, poor balance and difficulty performing routine activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we focus on identifying the root cause of knee pain instead of only temporary pain relief. Our physiotherapy programs are designed to reduce pain, improve knee mobility, strengthen muscles and support long-term recovery.

Common Causes of Knee Pain

  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Ligament injury
  • Meniscus injury
  • Sports injuries
  • Muscle weakness around the knee
  • Patellofemoral pain syndrome
  • Improper walking mechanics
  • Obesity and joint overload
  • Prolonged standing or overuse

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Knee pain while walking or climbing stairs
  • Swelling or stiffness in the knee joint
  • Difficulty squatting or sitting cross-legged
  • Clicking or locking sensation in the knee
  • Weakness or instability while walking
  • Pain after sports activity or exercise
  • Difficulty standing for long duration

Frequently Asked Questions

Can knee pain be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce knee pain, improve mobility, strengthen muscles and improve walking and daily activities depending on the cause of pain.

What causes knee pain?

Knee pain may be caused by arthritis, ligament injury, meniscus injury, muscle weakness, sports injury, poor posture, obesity or overuse of the knee joint.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for knee pain?

You should visit a physiotherapist if knee pain lasts more than one week, increases during walking or stair climbing, causes swelling or affects daily activities.
