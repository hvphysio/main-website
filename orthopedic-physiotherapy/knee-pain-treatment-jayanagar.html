Knee Pain Physiotherapy in Jayanagar, Bangalore

Knee pain is a common orthopedic problem that can affect walking, stair climbing, squatting, sitting, standing, sports activity and daily movement. It may develop due to arthritis, ligament injury, meniscus injury, patellofemoral pain, muscle weakness, poor posture, overuse, obesity, sports injury or age-related joint changes.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation, Jayanagar 9th Block, Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for knee pain by identifying the root cause of pain, stiffness, weakness or instability. Our treatment focuses on pain relief, joint mobility, muscle strengthening, balance correction, gait improvement and long-term knee function.

Common Causes of Knee Pain

Knee pain can come from the joint, cartilage, ligaments, tendons, muscles or surrounding soft tissues. The most common causes include knee osteoarthritis, patellofemoral pain syndrome, meniscus injury, ligament sprain, ACL or MCL injury, tendonitis, bursitis, IT band tightness, muscle imbalance, flat foot posture, sports injury and post-surgical weakness.

Symptoms of Knee Pain

  • Pain while walking, standing or climbing stairs
  • Pain while squatting or sitting cross-legged
  • Swelling, stiffness or heaviness around the knee
  • Clicking, locking or catching sensation
  • Weakness in thigh or knee muscles
  • Difficulty getting up from chair or floor
  • Pain around kneecap, inner knee or outer knee
  • Instability or feeling that the knee may give way
  • Reduced range of motion and poor walking pattern

Physiotherapy Treatment for Knee Pain

Physiotherapy treatment for knee pain depends on the exact cause, pain level, swelling, muscle strength, joint movement and functional limitation. At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation, we design a personalized knee rehabilitation plan after proper assessment.

  • Pain relief modalities such as IFT, TENS, ultrasound and moist heat when required
  • Manual therapy for joint stiffness and restricted knee movement
  • Soft tissue release for quadriceps, hamstrings, calf, IT band and surrounding muscles
  • Patellar mobilization and kneecap tracking correction
  • Quadriceps, hamstring and glute strengthening exercises
  • Balance, proprioception and gait training
  • Posture, walking and stair-climbing correction
  • Functional training for sitting, standing, squatting and daily activities
  • Home exercise program for long-term recovery and recurrence prevention

Advanced Knee Pain Treatment Techniques

For chronic knee pain, soft tissue tightness, sports injuries and stiffness-related problems, we may use advanced physiotherapy techniques depending on the patient’s condition.

  • KIASTM for soft tissue restriction and muscle tightness
  • DIASTM for advanced myofascial and fascial release
  • Myofascial release for pain, stiffness and movement restriction
  • Cupping therapy when suitable for muscle tightness and recovery
  • Trigger point release for painful muscle knots
  • Progressive strengthening and functional rehabilitation
  • Sports-specific knee rehabilitation for athletes and active individuals
