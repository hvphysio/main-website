What Causes Neck Pain?

Neck pain is a common problem caused by poor posture, prolonged mobile or computer use, cervical stiffness, muscle tightness, stress or cervical spine problems. It may affect daily activities, concentration, sleep and work performance.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for neck pain focused on posture correction, pain relief, cervical mobility and long-term rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Neck Pain

  • Poor posture and forward neck posture
  • Prolonged computer or mobile use
  • Cervical muscle tightness
  • Stress-related muscle tension
  • Cervical stiffness
  • Cervical spondylosis
  • Improper sleeping posture
  • Reduced cervical mobility

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Neck pain during movement
  • Neck stiffness and tightness
  • Pain while sitting or working
  • Reduced neck movement
  • Headache associated with neck pain
  • Shoulder tightness and discomfort
  • Pain radiating to shoulder or arm
  • Difficulty maintaining posture
