What Is Carpal Tunnel Syndrome?

Carpal tunnel syndrome occurs when the median nerve gets compressed at the wrist. It commonly causes wrist pain, numbness, tingling, weakness and difficulty using the hand during daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for carpal tunnel syndrome focused on reducing nerve compression, improving wrist mobility and restoring hand function.

Common Causes of Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

  • Prolonged typing and computer work
  • Repetitive hand and wrist movements
  • Wrist overuse strain
  • Poor wrist posture during work
  • Repetitive gripping activities
  • Hand and wrist muscle tightness
  • Weak wrist stabilizing muscles
  • Repetitive occupational stress

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Numbness or tingling in fingers
  • Wrist pain and hand discomfort
  • Weak grip strength
  • Pain during typing or gripping
  • Night pain or numbness
  • Difficulty holding objects
  • Hand weakness and fatigue
