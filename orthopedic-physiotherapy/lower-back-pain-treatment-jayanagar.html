What Causes Lower Back Pain?

Lower back pain is one of the most common orthopedic problems affecting people of all age groups. It may occur due to poor posture, prolonged sitting, muscle strain, spinal stiffness, lifting injury, weak core muscles, disc problems or sedentary lifestyle.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we focus on identifying the root cause of lower back pain and providing advanced physiotherapy rehabilitation for long-term pain relief and movement improvement.

Common Causes of Lower Back Pain

  • Poor sitting posture
  • Prolonged desk work
  • Muscle strain and tightness
  • Weak core muscles
  • Lumbar stiffness
  • Slip disc and disc bulge
  • Sciatica and nerve irritation
  • Improper lifting techniques
  • Reduced spinal mobility

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Lower back pain during sitting or walking
  • Back stiffness after rest
  • Pain while bending or lifting
  • Pain spreading to buttock or leg
  • Numbness or tingling in the leg
  • Difficulty sitting for long duration
  • Repeated episodes of lower back pain
