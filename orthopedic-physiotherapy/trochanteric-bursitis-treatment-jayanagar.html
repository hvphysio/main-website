What Is Trochanteric Bursitis?

Trochanteric bursitis is an inflammatory condition affecting the bursa located on the outer side of the hip. It commonly causes outer hip pain, tenderness and difficulty during walking, stair climbing or lying on the affected side.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for trochanteric bursitis focused on pain reduction, hip mobility, muscle balance and movement correction.

Common Causes of Trochanteric Bursitis

  • Repetitive walking or running stress
  • Hip muscle tightness and imbalance
  • Poor walking mechanics
  • Hip weakness and instability
  • Improper posture
  • Overuse strain injuries
  • Prolonged standing or activity
  • Hip joint stiffness

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Pain on the outer side of the hip
  • Pain during walking or stair climbing
  • Difficulty lying on the affected side
  • Hip tenderness and stiffness
  • Pain after prolonged standing
  • Reduced hip movement
  • Walking discomfort and imbalance
