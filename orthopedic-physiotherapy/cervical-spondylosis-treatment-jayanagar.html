What is Cervical Spondylosis?

Cervical spondylosis is a degenerative condition affecting the neck region of the spine. It may involve disc degeneration, joint stiffness, muscle tightness, reduced neck movement and irritation of nearby nerves. Many patients experience neck pain, shoulder discomfort, headache or tingling travelling into the arm.

Common Symptoms of Cervical Spondylosis

  • Neck pain and stiffness
  • Pain spreading to shoulder or upper back
  • Headache related to neck stiffness
  • Numbness or tingling in arm, hand or fingers
  • Burning pain or nerve pain in the arm
  • Reduced neck movement
  • Muscle tightness around neck and shoulder
  • Pain while using laptop or mobile for long duration
  • Weakness in arm or hand in some patients
  • Posture-related neck fatigue

Common Causes of Cervical Spondylosis and Neck Pain

  • Age-related cervical spine degeneration
  • Poor sitting posture
  • Forward head posture
  • Prolonged mobile or laptop use
  • Weak neck and upper back muscles
  • Cervical disc bulge or nerve compression
  • Muscle tightness and trigger points
  • Reduced shoulder and thoracic mobility
  • Improper pillow or sleeping posture

Advanced Physiotherapy Treatment for Cervical Spondylosis

Cervical spondylosis treatment should focus on reducing pain, improving neck mobility, correcting posture, reducing nerve irritation and strengthening the muscles that support the cervical spine.

  • Detailed physiotherapy assessment
  • Manual therapy and cervical mobilization
  • Soft tissue release for neck and shoulder muscles
  • Myofascial Release (MFR)
  • KIASTM and DIASTM techniques for soft tissue restriction
  • Dry Needling for trigger points and muscle tightness
  • IFT and TENS therapy for pain relief
  • Ultrasound therapy when required
  • Cervical traction when clinically indicated
  • Posture correction and ergonomic advice
  • Deep neck flexor strengthening
  • Scapular strengthening exercises
  • Nerve gliding exercises for arm symptoms
  • Home exercise program for long-term neck care

Frequently Asked Questions

Can cervical spondylosis be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce neck pain, stiffness, muscle tightness and nerve-related symptoms in many cervical spondylosis cases through manual therapy, posture correction, strengthening and rehabilitation exercises.

What are common symptoms of cervical spondylosis?

Common symptoms include neck pain, stiffness, headache, shoulder pain, arm pain, numbness, tingling, reduced neck movement and posture-related discomfort.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for neck pain?

You should visit a physiotherapist if neck pain lasts more than a few days, keeps recurring, spreads to the shoulder or arm, or is associated with numbness, tingling or weakness.
