What Is a Knee Ligament Injury?

Knee ligament injuries occur when the supporting ligaments of the knee become stretched, partially torn or injured due to twisting movements, sports injuries, falls or sudden impact. These injuries commonly cause pain, swelling, instability and difficulty walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for knee ligament injuries focused on pain reduction, knee stability, muscle strengthening and safe return to activity.

Common Causes of Knee Ligament Injury

  • Sports-related twisting injuries
  • Sudden knee movement or pivoting
  • Falls and trauma
  • Improper landing during sports
  • Weak knee stability muscles
  • Overuse and repetitive strain
  • Running and jumping injuries
  • Movement imbalance and poor mechanics

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Knee pain and swelling
  • Knee instability or giving way
  • Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
  • Reduced knee movement
  • Pain during weight-bearing
  • Difficulty during sports activity
  • Weakness around the knee joint
