What Is Postural Back Pain?

Postural back pain develops due to poor sitting posture, prolonged desk work, muscle imbalance and reduced spinal mobility. It commonly affects office workers, students, computer users and people with prolonged sitting habits.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for postural back pain focused on posture correction, spinal mobility, muscle strengthening and long-term pain prevention.

Common Causes of Postural Back Pain

  • Poor sitting posture
  • Long hours of desk work
  • Forward bending posture
  • Weak core muscles
  • Reduced spinal mobility
  • Improper workstation ergonomics
  • Muscle tightness and imbalance
  • Physical inactivity

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Back pain after prolonged sitting
  • Neck and upper back stiffness
  • Difficulty maintaining upright posture
  • Muscle tightness and fatigue
  • Back pain during desk work
  • Postural imbalance
  • Reduced flexibility and movement
