What Is Plantar Fasciitis?

Plantar fasciitis is a condition in which the plantar fascia tissue under the foot becomes irritated and painful. It commonly causes heel pain, foot stiffness and difficulty during walking, especially in the morning or after prolonged rest.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for plantar fasciitis focused on pain reduction, flexibility improvement, foot stability and movement correction.

Common Causes of Plantar Fasciitis

  • Prolonged standing or walking
  • Running and sports-related stress
  • Tight calf muscles
  • Flat feet or foot imbalance
  • Improper footwear
  • Reduced foot flexibility
  • Overuse strain injuries
  • Weight-bearing stress on the heel

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Heel pain during walking
  • Morning heel stiffness
  • Pain after prolonged standing
  • Foot tightness and discomfort
  • Difficulty walking comfortably
  • Pain after sports activity
  • Reduced foot flexibility and movement
