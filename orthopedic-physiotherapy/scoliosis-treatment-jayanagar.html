What Is Scoliosis?

Scoliosis is a spinal condition in which the spine curves sideways instead of maintaining a normal straight alignment. It may affect posture, spinal balance, shoulder level, walking pattern and overall body mechanics. Some patients may experience back pain, muscle tightness, fatigue or visible postural asymmetry.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for scoliosis focused on posture correction, spinal mobility, muscle balance, breathing mechanics and long-term spinal rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Scoliosis

  • Idiopathic scoliosis
  • Postural imbalance
  • Muscle weakness and imbalance
  • Congenital spinal conditions
  • Neuromuscular disorders
  • Poor posture during growth years
  • Spinal asymmetry and imbalance
  • Reduced spinal stability

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Uneven shoulders or hips
  • Visible spinal curvature
  • Back pain or spinal stiffness
  • Muscle tightness and fatigue
  • Poor posture and body imbalance
  • Difficulty standing for long duration
  • Reduced spinal flexibility
  • Breathing discomfort in severe cases
