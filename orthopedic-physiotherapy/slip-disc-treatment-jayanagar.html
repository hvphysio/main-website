Advanced Physiotherapy for Slip Disc and Sciatica

Slip disc is one of the most common causes of lower back pain and sciatica. It occurs when the spinal disc puts pressure on nearby nerves causing pain, numbness, tingling or weakness in the lower back and legs. Common Symptoms of Slip Disc

  • Lower back pain
  • Sciatica pain radiating to the leg
  • Numbness or tingling in legs
  • Pain while sitting or bending
  • Muscle weakness in legs
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Stiffness in lower back
  • Pain during prolonged sitting

Frequently Asked Questions

Can slip disc be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, many slip disc and disc bulge cases can improve with proper physiotherapy, posture correction, pain management and strengthening exercises. Treatment depends on the severity of disc compression and symptoms.

Is physiotherapy helpful for sciatica pain?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce sciatic nerve irritation, improve spinal movement, strengthen supporting muscles and reduce leg pain caused by nerve compression.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for slip disc?

You should visit a physiotherapist if back pain lasts more than one week, pain travels to the leg, or you feel numbness, tingling or weakness in the lower limb.
