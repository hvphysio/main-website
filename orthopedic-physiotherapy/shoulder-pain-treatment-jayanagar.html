Why Shoulder Pain Should Not Be Ignored

Shoulder pain can affect lifting, dressing, overhead activity, sleep and daily work. If ignored, shoulder stiffness and weakness may gradually worsen and reduce arm mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we focus on identifying the root cause of shoulder pain and creating personalized rehabilitation programs to improve mobility, reduce pain and restore shoulder function.

Common Causes of Shoulder Pain

  • Frozen shoulder
  • Rotator cuff injury
  • Shoulder impingement syndrome
  • Tendon inflammation
  • Sports injuries
  • Poor posture
  • Muscle weakness
  • Repetitive overhead activity
  • Shoulder joint stiffness

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Shoulder pain while lifting the arm
  • Difficulty reaching overhead
  • Shoulder stiffness and restricted movement
  • Pain while sleeping on the affected side
  • Weakness in the shoulder or arm
  • Clicking or discomfort during movement
  • Difficulty dressing or combing hair

Frequently Asked Questions

Can shoulder pain be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce shoulder pain, improve movement, strengthen muscles and improve daily activities depending on the cause of pain.

What causes shoulder pain?

Shoulder pain may be caused by frozen shoulder, rotator cuff injury, tendon inflammation, muscle weakness, sports injury, poor posture or repetitive overhead activities.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for shoulder pain?

You should visit a physiotherapist if shoulder pain lasts more than one week, restricts arm movement, increases during lifting or disturbs sleep.
