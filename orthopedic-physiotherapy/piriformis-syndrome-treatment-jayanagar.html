What Is Piriformis Syndrome?

Piriformis syndrome occurs when the piriformis muscle in the buttock region irritates or compresses the sciatic nerve. It commonly causes buttock pain, hip tightness, leg pain, tingling and discomfort during sitting or walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for piriformis syndrome focused on muscle relaxation, nerve mobility, pain reduction and movement correction.

Common Causes of Piriformis Syndrome

  • Prolonged sitting
  • Hip muscle tightness
  • Poor posture and movement mechanics
  • Overuse during walking or running
  • Weak hip and core muscles
  • Improper exercise technique
  • Pelvic imbalance
  • Sports-related muscle strain

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Buttock pain and tightness
  • Pain radiating down the leg
  • Tingling or numbness in the leg
  • Pain during prolonged sitting
  • Difficulty walking comfortably
  • Hip stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Sciatic nerve irritation symptoms
