What Is Frozen Shoulder?

Frozen shoulder, also called adhesive capsulitis, is a condition that causes shoulder pain, stiffness and gradual restriction of shoulder movement. Patients often find difficulty lifting the arm, reaching overhead, dressing, combing hair or sleeping on the affected side.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for frozen shoulder focused on pain relief, shoulder mobility restoration and long-term functional recovery.

Common Causes of Frozen Shoulder

  • Shoulder joint inflammation
  • Prolonged shoulder immobility
  • Rotator cuff injury
  • Post-surgical shoulder stiffness
  • Diabetes-related shoulder stiffness
  • Poor shoulder mobility
  • Previous shoulder injury
  • Muscle tightness around the shoulder

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Shoulder stiffness and reduced movement
  • Pain while lifting the arm
  • Difficulty reaching overhead
  • Pain while dressing or combing hair
  • Pain during sleep
  • Restricted arm mobility
  • Weakness around the shoulder joint
