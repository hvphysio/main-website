What Is SI Joint Dysfunction?

SI joint dysfunction affects the sacroiliac joint, which connects the spine and pelvis. It commonly causes lower back pain, buttock pain, pelvic discomfort and difficulty during standing, walking or sitting for long duration.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for SI joint dysfunction focused on pain reduction, pelvic stability, posture correction and movement improvement.

Common Causes of SI Joint Dysfunction

  • Poor posture and movement mechanics
  • Pelvic instability
  • Lower back muscle imbalance
  • Pregnancy-related pelvic stress
  • Sports and lifting injuries
  • Prolonged sitting
  • Weak core muscles
  • Improper walking mechanics

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Lower back pain near the pelvis
  • Buttock pain or pelvic pain
  • Pain while standing from sitting
  • Difficulty walking for long duration
  • Stiffness in lower back or pelvis
  • Pain during stair climbing
  • Instability while walking
