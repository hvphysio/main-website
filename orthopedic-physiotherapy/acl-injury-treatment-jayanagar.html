What Is ACL Injury?

ACL injury affects the anterior cruciate ligament of the knee and commonly occurs during sports, sudden twisting movements, jumping or landing injuries. ACL injuries may lead to knee instability, swelling, weakness and difficulty during walking or sports activity.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced ACL physiotherapy rehabilitation focused on pain reduction, knee stability, strength improvement, balance training and return to activity.

Common Causes of ACL Injury

  • Sports-related knee injury
  • Sudden twisting movement
  • Jumping and landing injuries
  • Football and cricket injuries
  • Weak knee stability
  • Improper movement mechanics
  • Sudden directional changes
  • Traumatic knee injury

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Knee instability or giving way sensation
  • Swelling around the knee
  • Pain during walking or movement
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced balance and confidence
  • Weakness around the knee
  • Difficulty during sports activity
