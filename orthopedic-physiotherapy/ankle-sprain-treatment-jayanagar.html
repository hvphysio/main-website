What Is an Ankle Sprain?

An ankle sprain occurs when the ligaments supporting the ankle become stretched or injured due to twisting movements, sports injuries, sudden imbalance or falls. It commonly causes pain, swelling, instability and difficulty walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for ankle sprains focused on pain reduction, ankle stability, balance training and safe return to activity.

Common Causes of Ankle Sprain

  • Sudden twisting of the ankle
  • Sports-related injuries
  • Running or jumping injuries
  • Uneven surface walking
  • Weak ankle stability muscles
  • Improper footwear
  • Balance and movement problems
  • Falls and trauma

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Ankle pain and swelling
  • Difficulty walking
  • Ankle instability or weakness
  • Pain during weight-bearing
  • Reduced ankle mobility
  • Difficulty during sports activity
  • Balance problems and instability
