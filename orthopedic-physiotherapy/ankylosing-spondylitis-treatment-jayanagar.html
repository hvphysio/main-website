What Is Ankylosing Spondylitis?

Ankylosing spondylitis is a chronic inflammatory condition that mainly affects the spine and sacroiliac joints. It may cause spinal stiffness, inflammatory back pain, posture changes and reduced spinal mobility. Symptoms often worsen after prolonged rest and improve with movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy rehabilitation for ankylosing spondylitis focused on spinal mobility, posture correction, breathing exercises and long-term functional improvement.

Common Symptoms of Ankylosing Spondylitis

  • Chronic lower back pain
  • Morning stiffness
  • Spinal stiffness and reduced flexibility
  • Pain near the sacroiliac joint
  • Poor posture and forward bending
  • Difficulty standing for long duration
  • Reduced chest expansion
  • Fatigue and movement restriction
