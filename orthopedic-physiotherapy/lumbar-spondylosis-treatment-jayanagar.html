What is Lumbar Spondylosis?

Lumbar spondylosis is a degenerative condition affecting the lower spine. It may involve disc degeneration, stiffness, reduced spinal mobility, muscle tightness and irritation of nearby nerves causing lower back pain and sometimes sciatica symptoms.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide assessment-based physiotherapy treatment for lumbar spondylosis focused on pain relief, posture correction, spinal mobility and long-term spine rehabilitation.

Common Symptoms of Lumbar Spondylosis

  • Lower back stiffness
  • Chronic lower back pain
  • Pain while standing or walking for long duration
  • Reduced spinal flexibility
  • Pain radiating to the buttock or leg
  • Sciatica symptoms
  • Difficulty bending forward or backward
  • Muscle tightness around lower back and hip

Common Causes of Lumbar Spondylosis

  • Age-related spinal degeneration
  • Poor posture
  • Weak core muscles
  • Prolonged sitting
  • Repetitive strain on lower back
  • Disc degeneration
  • Reduced spinal mobility
  • Improper lifting mechanics

Advanced Physiotherapy Treatment for Lumbar Spondylosis

Physiotherapy treatment for lumbar spondylosis should focus on reducing stiffness, improving movement, strengthening spinal support muscles and preventing progression of pain and mobility limitations.

  • Detailed physiotherapy assessment
  • Manual therapy and spinal mobilization
  • IFT and TENS therapy
  • Ultrasound therapy when required
  • Myofascial release
  • Stretching exercises for tight muscles
  • Core strengthening exercises
  • Posture correction and ergonomic training
  • Spinal stabilization exercises
  • Home exercise program for long-term spine care

Frequently Asked Questions

Can lumbar spondylosis be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce pain, improve spinal mobility, strengthen supporting muscles and improve posture in lumbar spondylosis patients.

Is lumbar spondylosis permanent?

Lumbar spondylosis is a degenerative condition, but symptoms can often be managed effectively with physiotherapy, exercise, posture correction and lifestyle changes.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for lumbar spondylosis?

You should visit a physiotherapist if lower back pain, stiffness or mobility restriction is affecting your daily activities, walking, sitting or sleep.
