What Is Flat Foot?

Flat foot, also called flat feet, is a condition in which the arch of the foot is reduced or absent. It may cause foot pain, walking imbalance, ankle strain, knee discomfort and posture-related movement problems.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for flat foot focused on improving foot mechanics, arch support, balance and walking efficiency.

Common Problems Associated with Flat Foot

  • Foot pain and fatigue
  • Arch weakness
  • Walking imbalance
  • Ankle instability
  • Knee pain related to foot posture
  • Reduced balance and stability
  • Improper weight distribution
  • Difficulty during prolonged standing

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Foot pain during walking
  • Heel or arch discomfort
  • Fatigue after prolonged standing
  • Walking imbalance
  • Ankle strain and instability
  • Difficulty during sports activity
  • Poor posture and movement control

Flat Foot Treatment FAQs

Can physiotherapy help flat foot?

Yes, physiotherapy helps improve foot strength, walking pattern, ankle stability and posture problems related to flat foot through exercises and rehabilitation programs.

What causes flat foot?

Flat foot may be caused by weak foot muscles, ligament laxity, prolonged standing, improper footwear, obesity, injury or poor foot biomechanics.

What is the best physiotherapy treatment for flat foot in Jayanagar Bangalore?

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation, treatment may include foot strengthening exercises, balance training, gait correction, stretching, manual therapy and rehabilitation programs.

Can flat foot cause knee pain or back pain?

Yes, flat foot can affect body alignment and may contribute to foot pain, ankle pain, knee pain, hip pain or lower back pain in some patients.
