What Is Tennis Elbow?

Tennis elbow, also called lateral epicondylitis, is a painful condition affecting the outer side of the elbow. It commonly develops due to repetitive wrist and forearm movements, overuse of gripping muscles, sports activity, computer work or lifting strain.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for tennis elbow focused on pain reduction, soft tissue recovery, grip strength improvement and long-term functional rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Tennis Elbow

  • Repetitive gripping or lifting
  • Overuse of wrist and forearm muscles
  • Sports activities such as tennis, badminton or gym training
  • Computer and mouse overuse
  • Poor lifting technique
  • Forearm muscle tightness
  • Repetitive household or work-related strain
  • Weak wrist and elbow stabilizing muscles

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Pain on the outer side of the elbow
  • Pain while gripping or lifting objects
  • Forearm pain and tightness
  • Difficulty opening bottles or doors
  • Pain during typing, mouse use or tool work
  • Weak grip strength
  • Pain that increases with wrist movement
