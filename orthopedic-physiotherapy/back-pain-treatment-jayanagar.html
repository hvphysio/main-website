Why Back Pain Should Not Be Ignored

Back pain is one of the most common reasons patients visit a physiotherapy clinic. It may start as mild stiffness or discomfort, but if ignored, it can become recurrent, affect sitting tolerance, disturb sleep, reduce mobility and interfere with daily work.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we focus on finding the root cause of back pain instead of giving only temporary relief. Our physiotherapy programs are designed to reduce pain, improve spinal mobility, correct posture and build long-term strength.

Common Causes of Back Pain

  • Poor sitting posture
  • Muscle strain or ligament stress
  • Weak core muscles
  • Slip disc or disc bulge
  • Sciatica or nerve irritation
  • Lumbar spondylosis
  • Prolonged sitting or desk work
  • Improper lifting technique
  • Reduced hip and spinal mobility

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Lower back pain lasting more than one week
  • Back stiffness after sitting or sleeping
  • Pain while bending, lifting or walking
  • Pain spreading to buttock, thigh or leg
  • Numbness or tingling in the leg
  • Repeated episodes of back pain
  • Difficulty sitting for long duration

Frequently Asked Questions

Can back pain be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, many back pain cases improve with physiotherapy, posture correction, manual therapy, strengthening exercises and rehabilitation.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for back pain?

You should visit a physiotherapist if back pain lasts more than one week, keeps recurring, increases while sitting or bending, or travels to the leg with numbness or tingling.

What causes lower back pain?

Lower back pain may be caused by poor posture, muscle strain, weak core muscles, slip disc, sciatica, lumbar spondylosis, prolonged sitting or incorrect lifting.
