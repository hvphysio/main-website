What Causes Hip Pain?

Hip pain may develop due to muscle tightness, hip arthritis, joint stiffness, sports injury, posture imbalance or reduced hip mobility. It can affect walking, standing, stair climbing and daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for hip pain focused on pain reduction, hip mobility, muscle strengthening and movement improvement.

Common Causes of Hip Pain

  • Hip joint stiffness
  • Hip arthritis
  • Muscle tightness and imbalance
  • Sports-related hip injury
  • Poor posture and movement mechanics
  • Reduced hip mobility
  • Walking imbalance
  • Overuse strain injuries

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Hip pain during walking or standing
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Hip stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Pain while sitting or standing for long duration
  • Groin pain or buttock pain
  • Difficulty during daily activities
  • Reduced balance and movement control
