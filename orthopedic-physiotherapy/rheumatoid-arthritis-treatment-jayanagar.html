What Is Rheumatoid Arthritis?

Rheumatoid arthritis is a chronic inflammatory autoimmune condition that affects multiple joints and may cause joint pain, swelling, stiffness and reduced mobility. It commonly affects the hands, knees, wrists, ankles and other joints symmetrically.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy rehabilitation for rheumatoid arthritis focused on pain reduction, joint mobility, muscle strengthening and improving daily function.

Common Symptoms of Rheumatoid Arthritis

  • Joint pain and swelling
  • Morning stiffness
  • Difficulty walking or using hands
  • Reduced joint mobility
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Inflammation in multiple joints
  • Joint tenderness and stiffness
  • Difficulty during daily activities
