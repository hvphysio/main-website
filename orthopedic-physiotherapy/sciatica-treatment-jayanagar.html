What is Sciatica?

Sciatica is a condition where pain travels along the sciatic nerve from the lower back to the buttock, thigh, calf or foot. It may feel like sharp pain, burning pain, tingling, numbness or electric shock-like discomfort in the leg.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide assessment-based physiotherapy treatment for sciatica by identifying the cause of nerve irritation and designing a personalized rehabilitation plan for pain relief and long-term recovery.

Common Causes of Sciatica

  • Slip disc or disc bulge
  • Lumbar spondylosis
  • Nerve root compression
  • Piriformis muscle tightness
  • Poor posture
  • Weak core muscles
  • Prolonged sitting
  • Muscle imbalance around the hip and lower back

Common Symptoms of Sciatica

  • Pain travelling from the lower back to the leg
  • Buttock, thigh, calf or foot pain
  • Numbness or tingling in the leg
  • Burning pain or electric shock-like pain
  • Pain while sitting for long duration
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Weakness in leg muscles

Frequently Asked Questions

Can sciatica be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, many sciatica cases improve with physiotherapy, posture correction, nerve mobility exercises and strengthening exercises.

What causes sciatica pain?

Sciatica pain may be caused by slip disc, disc bulge, lumbar spondylosis, nerve compression, piriformis tightness or poor posture.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for sciatica?

You should visit a physiotherapist if pain travels from the lower back to the buttock, thigh, calf or foot, or if you feel numbness, tingling or weakness in the leg.
