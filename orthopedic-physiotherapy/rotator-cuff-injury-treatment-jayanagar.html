What Is Rotator Cuff Injury?

Rotator cuff injury affects the muscles and tendons that stabilize the shoulder joint. It commonly causes shoulder pain, weakness and difficulty lifting the arm. Sports activity, repetitive overhead movement, trauma or poor shoulder mechanics may contribute to rotator cuff problems.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced physiotherapy treatment for rotator cuff injuries focused on pain reduction, mobility improvement, strengthening and long-term shoulder rehabilitation.

Common Causes of Rotator Cuff Injury

  • Sports-related shoulder injury
  • Repetitive overhead activity
  • Shoulder muscle weakness
  • Sudden lifting injury
  • Poor shoulder posture
  • Age-related tendon degeneration
  • Shoulder impingement syndrome
  • Rotator cuff tendon inflammation

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Attention

  • Shoulder pain while lifting the arm
  • Weakness during arm movement
  • Difficulty reaching overhead
  • Pain while sleeping on the affected side
  • Shoulder stiffness and restricted movement
  • Difficulty carrying objects
  • Pain during sports or exercise

Frequently Asked Questions

Can rotator cuff injuries be treated with physiotherapy?

Yes, physiotherapy can help reduce pain, improve shoulder strength, restore movement and improve shoulder function in many rotator cuff injury cases.

What are symptoms of rotator cuff injury?

Common symptoms include shoulder pain, weakness, pain while lifting the arm, difficulty reaching overhead and pain during sleep.

When should I visit a physiotherapist for shoulder injury?

You should visit a physiotherapist if shoulder pain limits arm movement, causes weakness or affects work, sports or daily activities.
