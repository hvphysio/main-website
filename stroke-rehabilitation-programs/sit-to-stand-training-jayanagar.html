Why Is Sit to Stand Training Important After Stroke?

Stroke can reduce leg strength, balance and coordination, making it difficult to stand up safely from a chair or bed. Sit to stand training helps improve lower limb strength, postural control, transfer ability and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on transfer recovery, standing balance and functional mobility improvement.

Common Difficulties During Sit to Stand After Stroke

  • Difficulty standing from sitting position
  • Weakness in the legs
  • Poor balance and postural control
  • Fear of falling during transfers
  • Difficulty shifting body weight
  • Reduced trunk stability
  • Reduced confidence during movement
  • Difficulty performing daily activities independently
