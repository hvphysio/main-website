Why Is Weight Bearing Training Important After Stroke?

After stroke, many patients avoid putting weight on the affected side because of weakness, poor balance and reduced confidence. Weight bearing training helps improve muscle activation, balance control, standing stability and walking ability.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on weight shifting, standing balance, walking recovery and functional independence.

Common Problems Related to Reduced Weight Bearing After Stroke

  • Difficulty standing independently
  • Avoiding weight on the affected leg
  • Poor balance and posture
  • Weakness in the affected side
  • Difficulty walking safely
  • Reduced confidence during movement
  • Increased risk of falls
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
