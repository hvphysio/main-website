What Is Muscle Re-education After Stroke?

Muscle re-education is a neuro physiotherapy approach used after stroke to retrain muscles and improve communication between the brain and body. It helps patients regain movement control, coordination, balance and functional mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on movement retraining, neuroplasticity, muscle activation and functional recovery.

Common Problems That Need Muscle Re-education

  • Difficulty moving the arm or leg
  • Weakness after stroke
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Abnormal movement patterns
  • Difficulty standing or walking
  • Reduced hand function and grip strength
  • Postural instability
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
