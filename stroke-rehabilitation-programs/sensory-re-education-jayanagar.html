What Is Sensory Re-education After Stroke?

Stroke can affect sensation, body awareness and movement control, leading to numbness, reduced touch sensation and difficulty coordinating movement. Sensory re-education helps retrain the brain to recognize and respond to sensory input more effectively.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on sensory recovery, movement coordination, balance improvement and functional independence.

Common Sensory Problems After Stroke

  • Numbness in the arm or leg
  • Reduced touch sensation
  • Difficulty recognizing body position
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Difficulty controlling movement
  • Reduced hand and finger awareness
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Reduced functional independence
