Why Is Hand Function Recovery Important After Stroke?

Stroke can affect arm and hand movement, making gripping, holding objects and performing daily activities difficult. Hand rehabilitation helps improve muscle control, coordination, grip strength and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on arm movement recovery, hand coordination, grip strength improvement and functional mobility.

Common Hand Problems After Stroke

  • Weakness in the hand and arm
  • Difficulty gripping or holding objects
  • Reduced finger movement and coordination
  • Muscle stiffness or spasticity
  • Difficulty performing fine motor activities
  • Reduced hand control during movement
  • Difficulty with self care activities
  • Reduced functional independence
