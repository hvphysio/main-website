What Is Activities of Daily Living Training After Stroke?

Activities of Daily Living training focuses on helping stroke patients regain independence in essential daily activities such as sitting, standing, dressing, walking, transferring and self care activities. It is an important part of stroke rehabilitation and functional recovery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on improving mobility, balance, coordination and functional independence during daily activities.

Common Difficulties in Daily Activities After Stroke

  • Difficulty standing or walking independently
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Difficulty transferring from bed or chair
  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Difficulty performing self care activities
  • Reduced hand function and grip strength
  • Fear of falling during movement
  • Reduced confidence and independence
