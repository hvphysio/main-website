Why Is Coordination Affected After Stroke?

Stroke can affect the brain’s ability to control smooth and coordinated movement, leading to difficulty with walking, balance, hand function and daily activities. Coordination training helps improve movement accuracy, stability and functional mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on coordination recovery, balance improvement and functional independence.

Common Coordination Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty controlling arm or leg movement
  • Poor balance during standing or walking
  • Difficulty performing smooth movements
  • Reduced hand and finger coordination
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Unsteady walking pattern
  • Reduced movement accuracy
  • Reduced functional independence
