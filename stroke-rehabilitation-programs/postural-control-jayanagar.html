Why Is Postural Control Important After Stroke?

Stroke can affect posture, trunk stability and balance control, making sitting, standing and walking difficult. Postural control training helps improve body alignment, movement stability and functional independence during daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on posture correction, trunk stability, balance improvement and mobility recovery.

Common Postural Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty sitting or standing independently
  • Loss of balance during movement
  • Leaning toward one side
  • Reduced trunk control and stability
  • Difficulty shifting body weight
  • Poor coordination during walking
  • Fear of falling during movement
  • Reduced functional independence
