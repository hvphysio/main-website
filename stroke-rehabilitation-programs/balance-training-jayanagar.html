Why Is Balance Training Important After Stroke?

Stroke can affect balance, posture and coordination, making standing and walking difficult. Balance training is an important part of stroke rehabilitation to improve stability, reduce fall risk and restore confidence during movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured balance rehabilitation programs focused on postural control, walking improvement and functional independence after stroke.

Common Balance Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty standing independently
  • Frequent loss of balance
  • Fear of falling during walking
  • Postural instability
  • Difficulty shifting body weight
  • Poor coordination during movement
  • Reduced walking confidence
  • Difficulty performing daily activities safely
