What Is Spasticity After Stroke?

Spasticity is increased muscle tightness or stiffness that commonly develops after stroke due to damage in the brain pathways controlling muscle movement. It can affect walking, arm movement, balance and daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured neuro physiotherapy programs focused on reducing muscle stiffness, improving movement control and restoring functional independence after stroke.

Common Problems Caused by Spasticity

  • Muscle tightness and stiffness
  • Difficulty moving the arm or leg
  • Reduced walking ability
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Abnormal posture and movement patterns
  • Difficulty with hand function and grip
  • Pain during movement
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
