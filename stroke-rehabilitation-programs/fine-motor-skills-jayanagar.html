Why Are Fine Motor Skills Affected After Stroke?

Stroke can affect finger movement, hand coordination and muscle control, making daily activities such as writing, buttoning clothes and holding objects difficult. Fine motor rehabilitation helps improve precision, coordination and functional hand use.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on finger coordination, grip control, hand movement recovery and functional independence.

Common Fine Motor Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty moving fingers independently
  • Reduced hand coordination
  • Weak grip strength
  • Difficulty holding small objects
  • Difficulty writing or using utensils
  • Reduced finger control during movement
  • Difficulty performing self care activities
  • Reduced functional independence
