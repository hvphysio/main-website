Why Does Muscle Weakness Occur After Stroke?

Stroke can reduce communication between the brain and muscles, leading to weakness, reduced movement control and difficulty performing daily activities. Muscle strengthening rehabilitation is essential to improve movement, walking ability and functional recovery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on muscle activation, strengthening, balance improvement and mobility recovery.

Common Problems Related to Muscle Weakness After Stroke

  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Difficulty standing or walking
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Fatigue during movement
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced grip strength and hand function
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Reduced confidence during movement
