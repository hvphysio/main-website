What Is Paralysis Recovery After Stroke?

Paralysis after stroke occurs when one side of the body becomes weak or difficult to move because of damage to the brain areas controlling movement. Stroke rehabilitation focuses on helping the patient regain muscle control, improve mobility and perform daily activities more independently.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured paralysis recovery programs after stroke with neuro physiotherapy, movement retraining, strengthening, balance training and functional rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Stroke Paralysis

  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Difficulty moving the arm or leg
  • Difficulty standing or walking
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Muscle stiffness or spasticity
  • Reduced hand function and grip strength
  • Difficulty with transfers and daily activities
  • Reduced confidence and functional independence
