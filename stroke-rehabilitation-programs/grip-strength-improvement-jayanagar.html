Why Is Grip Strength Reduced After Stroke?

Stroke can weaken the muscles of the hand and fingers, making gripping, holding and controlling objects difficult. Grip strength rehabilitation helps improve hand control, coordination and independence during daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on hand strengthening, finger coordination, movement recovery and functional hand use.

Common Grip Problems After Stroke

  • Weak grip while holding objects
  • Difficulty opening or closing the hand
  • Reduced finger strength and coordination
  • Difficulty carrying daily use objects
  • Reduced hand control during movement
  • Difficulty performing self care activities
  • Muscle stiffness in the hand
  • Reduced functional independence
