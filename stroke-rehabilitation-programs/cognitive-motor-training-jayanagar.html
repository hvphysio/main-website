What Is Cognitive Motor Training After Stroke?

Stroke can affect both physical movement and cognitive functions such as attention, planning and movement control. Cognitive motor training combines physical rehabilitation with mental task based exercises to improve coordination, mobility and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on movement recovery, coordination improvement, balance training and cognitive motor rehabilitation.

Common Cognitive Motor Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty concentrating during movement
  • Poor coordination and balance
  • Difficulty performing dual task activities
  • Reduced movement planning and control
  • Difficulty performing daily activities safely
  • Reduced walking confidence
  • Slow reaction and movement response
  • Reduced functional independence
