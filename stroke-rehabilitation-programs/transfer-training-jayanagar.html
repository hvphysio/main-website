Why Is Transfer Training Important After Stroke?

Stroke can affect balance, strength and coordination, making transfers difficult. Transfer training helps patients safely move between bed, chair, wheelchair and standing positions while improving confidence and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on transfer safety, mobility recovery and independent movement training.

Common Transfer Difficulties After Stroke

  • Difficulty moving from bed to chair
  • Difficulty standing from sitting position
  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Fear of falling during transfers
  • Reduced trunk stability
  • Difficulty shifting body weight
  • Reduced functional independence
