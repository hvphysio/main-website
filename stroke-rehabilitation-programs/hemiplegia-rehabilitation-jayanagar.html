What Is Hemiplegia After Stroke?

Hemiplegia is weakness or paralysis of one side of the body, commonly seen after stroke. It may affect the arm, leg, trunk, balance, walking ability and hand function, making daily activities difficult.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured hemiplegia rehabilitation focused on muscle activation, movement retraining, balance improvement, gait training and functional recovery.

Common Problems in Hemiplegia

  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Difficulty standing or walking
  • Reduced arm and hand movement
  • Poor balance and postural control
  • Muscle stiffness or spasticity
  • Difficulty with transfers and bed mobility
  • Reduced grip strength and hand function
  • Difficulty performing daily activities independently
