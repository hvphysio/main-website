What Is Walking Re-education After Stroke?

Walking re-education is a stroke rehabilitation program focused on retraining proper walking patterns after stroke. It helps improve step control, balance, posture, coordination and confidence during walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured walking rehabilitation programs focused on gait correction, movement control and functional mobility recovery after stroke.

Common Walking Difficulties After Stroke

  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Dragging of the affected leg
  • Reduced walking speed and endurance
  • Poor balance during walking
  • Abnormal gait pattern
  • Difficulty turning or changing direction
  • Fear of falling during movement
  • Reduced confidence while walking
