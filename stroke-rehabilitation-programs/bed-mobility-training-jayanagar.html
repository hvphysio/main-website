Why Is Bed Mobility Training Important After Stroke?

Stroke can make simple movements in bed difficult because of weakness, reduced coordination and poor balance control. Bed mobility training helps patients improve rolling, repositioning, sitting up and movement confidence during recovery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on bed mobility, transfer training, balance improvement and functional independence.

Common Bed Mobility Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty turning in bed
  • Difficulty sitting up independently
  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Difficulty repositioning in bed
  • Reduced trunk stability
  • Fear of movement and falling
  • Reduced functional independence
