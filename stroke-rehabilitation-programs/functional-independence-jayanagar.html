Why Is Functional Independence Important After Stroke?

Stroke can affect movement, balance, coordination and daily activities, making patients dependent on caregivers. Functional independence training helps improve mobility, confidence and the ability to perform daily tasks safely and independently.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on improving functional mobility, balance, walking and independence during daily activities.

Common Functional Difficulties After Stroke

  • Difficulty standing or walking independently
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Difficulty with transfers and mobility
  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Reduced hand function and grip strength
  • Fear of falling during movement
  • Reduced confidence and independence
