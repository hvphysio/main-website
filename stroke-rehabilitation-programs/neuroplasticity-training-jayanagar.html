What Is Neuroplasticity Training After Stroke?

Neuroplasticity refers to the brain’s ability to reorganize and form new neural connections after stroke. Neuroplasticity training uses repetitive, task specific rehabilitation exercises to help the brain relearn movement, coordination and functional activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on neuroplasticity based movement recovery, balance improvement and functional independence.
