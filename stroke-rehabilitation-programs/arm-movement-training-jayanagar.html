Why Is Arm Movement Training Important After Stroke?

Stroke can reduce arm strength, movement control and coordination, making lifting, reaching and daily activities difficult. Arm movement training helps improve shoulder mobility, muscle activation and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured stroke rehabilitation programs focused on arm movement recovery, muscle re-education, coordination and functional mobility.

Common Arm Problems After Stroke

  • Weakness in the shoulder and arm
  • Difficulty lifting or reaching
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Muscle stiffness or spasticity
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Reduced coordination during movement
  • Pain during arm movement
  • Reduced functional independence
