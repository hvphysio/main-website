What Is Gait Training After Stroke?

Gait training is a specialized stroke rehabilitation program focused on improving walking ability, movement coordination, balance and mobility after stroke. It helps patients regain safer and more independent walking patterns.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured gait rehabilitation programs focused on walking recovery, posture correction, balance improvement and functional mobility.

Common Walking Problems After Stroke

  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Dragging of the affected leg
  • Poor balance during walking
  • Reduced walking speed and endurance
  • Abnormal walking pattern
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Fear of falling while walking
  • Reduced confidence during movement
