What Is Parkinson’s Disease?

Parkinson’s disease is a neurological condition that affects movement, balance, coordination and muscle control. Patients commonly experience tremors, stiffness, slow movement, posture problems and difficulty walking.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy and Parkinson’s rehabilitation focused on improving mobility, walking ability, balance and independence in daily activities.

Common Symptoms of Parkinson’s Disease

  • Tremors in hands or legs
  • Slow movement and stiffness
  • Difficulty walking
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Reduced arm swing while walking
  • Postural instability
  • Muscle tightness
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
