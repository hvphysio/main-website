What Is Bell’s Palsy?

Bell’s palsy is a condition that causes sudden weakness or paralysis of facial muscles due to facial nerve involvement. Patients may experience facial drooping, difficulty closing the eye, facial asymmetry, speech difficulty and reduced facial expressions.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy for Bell’s palsy focused on improving facial muscle activation, symmetry and nerve recovery.

Common Symptoms of Bell’s Palsy

  • Facial drooping on one side
  • Difficulty closing the eye
  • Facial muscle weakness
  • Difficulty smiling properly
  • Speech difficulty
  • Facial tightness or discomfort
  • Reduced facial expressions
  • Difficulty eating or drinking
