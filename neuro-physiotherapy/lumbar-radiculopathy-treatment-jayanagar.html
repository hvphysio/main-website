What Is Lumbar Radiculopathy?

Lumbar radiculopathy is a condition caused by compression or irritation of spinal nerves in the lower back region. Patients may experience lower back pain, radiating leg pain, numbness, tingling, weakness and difficulty walking or standing for long periods.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on reducing nerve compression, improving spinal mobility and restoring lower limb function.

Common Symptoms of Lumbar Radiculopathy

  • Lower back pain
  • Radiating leg pain
  • Numbness and tingling in leg or foot
  • Weakness in lower limb
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Calf tightness and discomfort
  • Pain during bending or sitting
  • Reduced lower limb coordination
