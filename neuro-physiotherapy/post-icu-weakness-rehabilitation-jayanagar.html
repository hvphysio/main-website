What Is Post ICU Weakness?

Post ICU weakness is a condition where patients experience muscle weakness, reduced endurance, balance problems and mobility limitations after prolonged ICU stay, severe illness, surgery or ventilation support. Patients often require structured physiotherapy rehabilitation to regain strength and independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving muscle strength, walking ability, balance, endurance and functional recovery after ICU discharge.

Common Problems After ICU Stay

  • Generalized muscle weakness
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Fatigue and reduced endurance
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Loss of physical conditioning
