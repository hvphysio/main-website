What Is a Balance Disorder?

Balance disorders are conditions that affect body stability, coordination and controlled movement. Patients may experience dizziness, instability, unsteady walking and difficulty maintaining posture during daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving balance, coordination, posture and walking stability.

Common Symptoms of Balance Disorders

  • Unsteady walking
  • Frequent loss of balance
  • Dizziness and instability
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Coordination problems
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
