What Is Stroke Rehabilitation?

Stroke rehabilitation is a structured neuro physiotherapy program designed to help patients recover movement, strength, balance, walking ability and independence after a stroke. A stroke may cause weakness on one side of the body, paralysis, poor coordination, stiffness, difficulty walking, balance problems and reduced daily function.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced stroke rehabilitation focused on improving mobility, muscle control, gait, balance and functional independence through personalized neuro physiotherapy programs.

Common Problems After Stroke

  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Hemiplegia or hemiparesis
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Muscle stiffness or spasticity
  • Shoulder pain after stroke
  • Reduced hand function and grip strength
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Fear of falling and reduced confidence

Symptoms That Need Neuro Physiotherapy Attention

  • Unable to walk independently after stroke
  • Dragging of foot while walking
  • Weakness in arm, hand or leg
  • Difficulty maintaining balance
  • Stiffness in shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee or ankle
  • Poor coordination during movement
  • Difficulty standing from sitting
  • Reduced confidence in walking or climbing stairs
