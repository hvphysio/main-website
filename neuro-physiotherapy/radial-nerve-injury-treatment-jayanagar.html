What Is Radial Nerve Injury?

Radial nerve injury occurs when the radial nerve gets compressed, stretched or damaged, leading to weakness in wrist and finger movements. Patients may experience wrist drop, numbness, reduced grip strength and difficulty lifting the wrist or fingers.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving nerve recovery, muscle activation and upper limb function.

Common Symptoms of Radial Nerve Injury

  • Wrist drop
  • Difficulty lifting the wrist
  • Weak grip strength
  • Numbness in hand or forearm
  • Finger extension weakness
  • Reduced hand coordination
  • Upper limb weakness
  • Movement difficulty during daily activities
