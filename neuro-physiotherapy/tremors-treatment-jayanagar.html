What Are Tremors?

Tremors are involuntary rhythmic shaking movements that commonly affect the hands, arms, head or legs. Tremors may occur due to neurological disorders, movement disorders or nerve dysfunction, affecting balance, coordination and daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving movement control, balance, coordination and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Tremors

  • Shaking movements in hands or limbs
  • Difficulty holding objects
  • Reduced hand coordination
  • Balance and walking instability
  • Difficulty performing fine motor tasks
  • Movement control problems
  • Reduced confidence during activities
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
