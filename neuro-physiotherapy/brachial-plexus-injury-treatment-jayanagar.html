What Is Brachial Plexus Injury?

Brachial plexus injury occurs when the network of nerves controlling the shoulder, arm and hand gets stretched, compressed or injured. Patients may experience weakness, numbness, pain, reduced grip strength and movement difficulty in the upper limb.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving nerve recovery, muscle activation, mobility and upper limb function.

Common Symptoms of Brachial Plexus Injury

  • Arm or shoulder weakness
  • Numbness and tingling
  • Reduced grip strength
  • Difficulty lifting the arm
  • Muscle wasting
  • Pain and nerve discomfort
  • Reduced hand coordination
  • Movement limitations in upper limb
