What Is Ulnar Nerve Injury?

Ulnar nerve injury occurs when the ulnar nerve gets compressed, stretched or damaged, commonly affecting hand function and finger movements. Patients may experience numbness, tingling, weakness and reduced grip strength in the hand.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving nerve recovery, hand function and upper limb mobility.

Common Symptoms of Ulnar Nerve Injury

  • Numbness in ring and little finger
  • Tingling sensation in hand
  • Weak grip strength
  • Difficulty holding objects
  • Hand muscle weakness
  • Reduced finger coordination
  • Upper limb discomfort
  • Difficulty performing daily hand activities
