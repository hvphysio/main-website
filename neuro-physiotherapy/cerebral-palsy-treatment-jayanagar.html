What Is Cerebral Palsy?

Cerebral palsy is a neurological condition affecting movement, posture, balance and muscle coordination due to brain injury or abnormal brain development. Patients may experience muscle stiffness, weakness, delayed motor development and walking difficulties.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving mobility, balance, posture and functional independence for children and adults with cerebral palsy.

Common Symptoms of Cerebral Palsy

  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking
  • Delayed motor milestones
  • Postural instability
  • Weakness in limbs
  • Reduced mobility and flexibility
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
