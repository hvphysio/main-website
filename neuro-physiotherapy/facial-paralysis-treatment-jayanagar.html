What Is Facial Paralysis?

Facial paralysis is a condition that causes weakness or loss of movement in facial muscles due to nerve involvement. Patients may experience facial drooping, difficulty smiling, eye closure problems, speech difficulty and facial asymmetry.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving facial muscle activation, symmetry, coordination and neurological recovery.

Common Symptoms of Facial Paralysis

  • Facial drooping on one side
  • Difficulty smiling properly
  • Weak facial muscle control
  • Difficulty closing the eye
  • Speech difficulty
  • Reduced facial expressions
  • Difficulty eating or drinking
  • Facial tightness or discomfort
