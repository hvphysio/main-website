What Is Muscle Weakness?

Muscle weakness is a condition where the muscles are unable to generate normal strength for movement, posture, walking or daily activities. It may occur due to neurological conditions, stroke, nerve injury, spinal cord problems, prolonged inactivity or muscle imbalance.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving strength, balance, coordination, walking ability and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Muscle Weakness

  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced strength in arms or legs
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Fatigue during daily activities
  • Reduced grip strength
  • Difficulty lifting objects
  • Reduced mobility confidence
