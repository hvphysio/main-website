What Is Myelopathy?

Myelopathy is a neurological condition caused by compression or damage to the spinal cord, commonly affecting movement, balance, coordination and muscle strength. Cervical and lumbar spinal degeneration are common causes of myelopathy.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving mobility, posture, balance and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Myelopathy

  • Weakness in arms or legs
  • Difficulty walking
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Numbness and tingling
  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Difficulty with hand coordination
  • Neck or back discomfort
  • Reduced mobility and stability
