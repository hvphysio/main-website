What Is Quadriplegia?

Quadriplegia, also called tetraplegia, is a neurological condition involving paralysis or severe weakness affecting all four limbs and the trunk, commonly caused by spinal cord injury or neurological disorders. Patients may experience severe movement limitations, reduced balance, weakness and difficulty performing daily activities independently.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy and quadriplegia rehabilitation focused on improving mobility, posture, muscle activation and functional independence.

Common Problems in Quadriplegia

  • Weakness in all four limbs
  • Difficulty sitting or maintaining posture
  • Poor balance and trunk control
  • Reduced mobility and coordination
  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Difficulty with transfers and positioning
  • Reduced functional independence
  • Risk of pressure sores and complications
