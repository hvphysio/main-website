What Is Hemiplegia?

Hemiplegia is paralysis or severe weakness affecting one side of the body, commonly caused by stroke, brain injury or neurological disorders. Patients may experience difficulty walking, standing, balancing, using the arm or performing daily activities independently.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy and hemiplegia rehabilitation focused on improving strength, walking ability, balance and functional independence.

Common Problems in Hemiplegia

  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Difficulty using hand and arm
  • Reduced confidence during movement
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Fear of falling
