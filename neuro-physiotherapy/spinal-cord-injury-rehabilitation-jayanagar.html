What Is Spinal Cord Injury?

Spinal cord injury is a neurological condition caused by damage to the spinal cord due to trauma, accidents or medical conditions. Patients may experience weakness, paralysis, balance problems, reduced sensation and mobility limitations depending on the level of injury.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving mobility, muscle activation, balance and functional independence.

Common Problems After Spinal Cord Injury

  • Weakness or paralysis
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Mobility limitations
  • Reduced sensation in limbs
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Postural instability
