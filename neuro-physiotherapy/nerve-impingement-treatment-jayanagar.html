What Is Nerve Impingement?

Nerve impingement occurs when a nerve gets compressed or irritated by surrounding muscles, joints, discs or soft tissues. It may cause pain, tingling, numbness, weakness and movement difficulty in different parts of the body.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on reducing nerve irritation, improving mobility and restoring functional movement.

Common Symptoms of Nerve Impingement

  • Numbness and tingling
  • Burning nerve pain
  • Weakness in arm or leg
  • Reduced grip strength
  • Radiating pain
  • Muscle tightness
  • Difficulty walking or lifting
  • Reduced mobility and flexibility
