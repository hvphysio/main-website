What Is a Gait Abnormality?

Gait abnormality refers to an abnormal walking pattern caused by neurological, muscular or balance-related conditions. Patients may experience difficulty walking, instability, limping, reduced coordination and movement control problems.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving walking pattern, balance, coordination and mobility independence.

Common Symptoms of Gait Abnormalities

  • Difficulty walking normally
  • Unsteady walking pattern
  • Frequent imbalance or falls
  • Reduced coordination
  • Weakness in lower limbs
  • Slow walking speed
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced mobility confidence
