What Is Guillain-Barré Syndrome?

Guillain-Barré Syndrome, also called GBS, is a neurological condition that affects peripheral nerves and may cause sudden muscle weakness, numbness, tingling, balance problems and difficulty walking. After medical management, many patients require structured neuro physiotherapy to regain strength, mobility and independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving muscle strength, walking ability, balance, coordination and functional recovery after Guillain-Barré Syndrome.

Common Problems After Guillain-Barré Syndrome

  • Muscle weakness in arms or legs
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Numbness and tingling sensation
  • Fatigue and reduced endurance
  • Reduced grip strength
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced confidence during movement
