What Is Multiple Sclerosis?

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is a neurological condition affecting the brain and spinal cord, leading to weakness, fatigue, balance problems, muscle stiffness and mobility difficulties. Symptoms may vary depending on the severity and progression of the condition.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving mobility, balance, coordination and functional independence for patients with multiple sclerosis.

Common Symptoms of Multiple Sclerosis

  • Muscle weakness
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking
  • Fatigue and reduced endurance
  • Muscle stiffness and spasticity
  • Numbness and tingling
  • Reduced mobility and flexibility
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
