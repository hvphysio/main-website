What Is Fall Prevention Training?

Fall prevention training is a specialized neuro physiotherapy program designed to improve balance, coordination, posture and walking stability in individuals at risk of falls due to neurological or mobility-related conditions.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving movement confidence, reducing falls risk and enhancing functional independence.

Who Needs Fall Prevention Training?

  • Patients with balance disorders
  • People with gait instability
  • Neurological rehabilitation patients
  • Elderly individuals with falls risk
  • Patients with weakness or coordination problems
  • People with dizziness or vertigo
  • Stroke rehabilitation patients
  • Patients with postural instability
