What Is Sciatica?

Sciatica is a condition caused by irritation or compression of the sciatic nerve, commonly leading to lower back pain, radiating leg pain, numbness, tingling and weakness in the lower limb. Prolonged sitting, slip disc and lumbar nerve compression are common causes of sciatica.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on reducing nerve irritation, improving spinal mobility and restoring lower limb function.

Common Causes of Sciatica

  • Slip Disc (Lumbar Disc Herniation)
  • Lumbar Radiculopathy
  • Nerve Root Compression
  • Degenerative Disc Disease
  • Lumbar Spondylosis
  • Prolonged Sitting
  • Poor Posture
  • Spinal Canal Narrowing

Common Symptoms of Sciatica

  • Pain radiating from back to foot
  • Burning sensation in leg
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Pain while getting up from sitting
  • Radiating leg pain
  • Lower back pain
  • Numbness and tingling in leg or foot
  • Weakness in lower limb
  • Difficulty sitting for long periods
  • Pain during walking or bending
  • Calf tightness and discomfort
  • Reduced mobility and flexibility

When Should You Seek Physiotherapy For Sciatica?

You should consult a physiotherapist if you experience persistent leg pain, numbness, tingling, weakness, difficulty walking, pain while sitting, or symptoms that interfere with daily activities. Early physiotherapy intervention can help reduce nerve irritation and improve recovery.
