What Is Nerve Compression?

Nerve compression occurs when a nerve gets pressed by surrounding muscles, joints, discs or soft tissues. It may lead to pain, tingling, numbness, weakness and movement difficulty affecting daily activities and mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on reducing nerve pressure, improving mobility and restoring normal movement patterns.

Common Symptoms of Nerve Compression

  • Numbness and tingling sensation
  • Burning or radiating nerve pain
  • Weakness in muscles
  • Reduced grip strength
  • Pain during movement
  • Muscle tightness and stiffness
  • Difficulty walking or lifting
  • Reduced flexibility and coordination
