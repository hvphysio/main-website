What Is Foot Drop?

Foot drop is a condition where a person has difficulty lifting the front part of the foot due to nerve injury, muscle weakness or neurological disorders. Patients may drag the foot while walking, trip frequently and experience gait imbalance.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced neuro physiotherapy focused on improving gait, ankle strength, balance and lower limb function.

Common Symptoms of Foot Drop

  • Difficulty lifting the foot
  • Foot dragging during walking
  • Frequent tripping
  • Ankle weakness
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Numbness in lower leg or foot
  • Abnormal walking pattern
