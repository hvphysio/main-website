Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Hospitalization?

Elderly individuals commonly experience weakness, reduced mobility, balance problems and difficulty performing daily activities after hospitalization. Physiotherapy rehabilitation is essential to restore strength, endurance, walking ability and functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced post hospital rehabilitation focused on improving mobility, balance, walking ability and overall physical recovery.
