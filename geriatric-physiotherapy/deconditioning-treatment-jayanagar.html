What Is Deconditioning?

Deconditioning is a decline in physical strength, endurance and functional ability caused by prolonged inactivity, illness, hospitalization or aging. It commonly affects elderly individuals and may lead to difficulty walking, fatigue, weakness and reduced independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on improving strength, endurance, balance, mobility and overall functional recovery.

Common Symptoms of Deconditioning

  • Generalized body weakness
  • Reduced endurance
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Fatigue during daily activities
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced mobility confidence
