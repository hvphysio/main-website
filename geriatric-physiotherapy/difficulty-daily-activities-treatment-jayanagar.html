Why Do Elderly Individuals Experience Difficulty in Daily Activities?

Elderly individuals commonly experience difficulty performing daily activities due to weakness, reduced mobility, balance problems, neurological conditions, joint pain or post-surgical recovery. These problems may affect independence and quality of life.
