What Causes Difficulty Walking?

Difficulty walking in elderly individuals may occur due to muscle weakness, joint pain, balance disorders, neurological conditions, arthritis, reduced endurance or post-surgical weakness. It may affect independence and increase the risk of falls.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on improving walking ability, gait pattern, balance, coordination and overall mobility.

Common Symptoms Associated with Difficulty Walking

  • Slow or unsteady walking
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced walking endurance
  • Weakness in legs
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced mobility confidence
