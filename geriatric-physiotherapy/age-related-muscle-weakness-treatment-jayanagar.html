What Is Age Related Muscle Weakness?

Age related muscle weakness is a gradual reduction in muscle strength and endurance commonly seen in elderly individuals. It may affect walking, balance, mobility, posture and the ability to perform daily activities independently.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on improving muscle strength, balance, endurance, mobility and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Age Related Muscle Weakness

  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Generalized body weakness
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Fatigue during daily activities
  • Reduced endurance and mobility
  • Fear of falling
  • Difficulty performing functional activities
