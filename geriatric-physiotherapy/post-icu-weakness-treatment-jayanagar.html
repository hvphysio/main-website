Why Does Weakness Occur After ICU Stay?

Elderly individuals commonly experience generalized weakness, reduced endurance, balance problems and difficulty walking after prolonged ICU stay or critical illness. Muscle weakness and reduced mobility may significantly affect functional independence and recovery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced post ICU rehabilitation focused on improving strength, mobility, balance, endurance and walking ability.

Common Problems After ICU Stay

  • Generalized body weakness
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced endurance and stamina
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced functional independence
