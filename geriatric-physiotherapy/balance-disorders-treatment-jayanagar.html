What Are Balance Disorders?

Balance disorders are conditions that affect stability, coordination and safe movement. Elderly individuals may experience imbalance, dizziness, unsteady walking and increased risk of falls due to weakness, neurological conditions or reduced mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on improving balance, coordination, walking ability and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Balance Disorders

  • Unsteady walking
  • Frequent imbalance
  • Fear of falling
  • Dizziness during movement
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Coordination problems
  • Frequent falls risk
