What Is Parkinson's Disease?

Parkinson's disease is a neurological condition affecting movement, balance, coordination and muscle control. Elderly individuals commonly experience tremors, slow movements, stiffness, walking difficulty and reduced functional independence.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced Parkinson's rehabilitation focused on improving balance, gait, posture, mobility and overall functional recovery.

Common Symptoms of Parkinson's Disease

  • Tremors in hands or legs
  • Slow movements
  • Difficulty walking
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Muscle stiffness and rigidity
  • Postural instability
  • Reduced mobility and endurance
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
