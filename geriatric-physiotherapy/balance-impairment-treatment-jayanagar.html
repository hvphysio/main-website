What Is Balance Impairment?

Balance impairment is a condition where individuals experience instability, poor coordination and difficulty maintaining body balance during standing or walking. It commonly affects elderly individuals due to weakness, neurological conditions, reduced mobility or aging.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced balance rehabilitation focused on improving stability, coordination, mobility and falls prevention.

Common Symptoms of Balance Impairment

  • Unsteady walking
  • Frequent imbalance
  • Fear of falling
  • Difficulty standing independently
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Coordination problems
  • Walking instability
  • Frequent falls risk
