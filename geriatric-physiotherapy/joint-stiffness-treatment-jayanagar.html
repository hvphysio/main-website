What Is Joint Stiffness?

Joint stiffness is a condition where joints become less flexible and difficult to move smoothly. It commonly affects elderly individuals due to aging, osteoarthritis, inactivity or reduced mobility, leading to discomfort and difficulty performing daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on improving joint mobility, flexibility, strength and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Joint Stiffness

  • Difficulty moving joints freely
  • Morning stiffness
  • Reduced flexibility
  • Pain during movement
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Reduced mobility and endurance
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Reduced balance and coordination
