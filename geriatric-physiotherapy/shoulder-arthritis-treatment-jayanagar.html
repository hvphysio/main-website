What Is Shoulder Arthritis?

Shoulder arthritis is a degenerative condition affecting the shoulder joint cartilage, commonly causing pain, stiffness and difficulty moving the arm. It is more common in elderly individuals and may affect daily activities such as lifting, dressing and overhead movements.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on reducing shoulder pain, improving mobility, restoring strength and enhancing functional arm movement.

Common Symptoms of Shoulder Arthritis

  • Shoulder pain during movement
  • Joint stiffness
  • Difficulty lifting the arm
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Pain during overhead activities
  • Weakness around the shoulder
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Grinding or clicking sensation
