What Are Gait Abnormalities?

Gait abnormalities are abnormal walking patterns caused by weakness, balance disorders, neurological conditions, joint pain or reduced mobility. Elderly individuals commonly experience instability, slow walking and difficulty maintaining safe movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced gait rehabilitation focused on improving walking pattern, balance, coordination and overall mobility.

Common Symptoms of Gait Abnormalities

  • Unsteady walking pattern
  • Slow walking speed
  • Difficulty maintaining balance
  • Frequent stumbling
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty walking independently
  • Reduced coordination during movement
