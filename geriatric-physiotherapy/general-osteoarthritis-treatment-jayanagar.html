What Is Osteoarthritis?

Osteoarthritis is a degenerative joint condition caused by gradual wear and tear of joint cartilage. It commonly affects elderly individuals and may involve the knees, hips, shoulders, spine and other joints, leading to pain, stiffness and difficulty performing daily activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on reducing joint pain, improving mobility, increasing strength and enhancing functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Osteoarthritis

  • Joint pain during movement
  • Morning stiffness
  • Reduced joint mobility
  • Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
  • Swelling around joints
  • Joint cracking or grinding sensation
  • Reduced balance and mobility
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
