What Are Coordination Problems?

Coordination problems occur when individuals experience difficulty controlling body movements smoothly and accurately. Elderly individuals may develop coordination issues due to neurological conditions, weakness, balance impairment or reduced mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced coordination rehabilitation focused on improving balance, movement control, gait and functional independence.

Common Symptoms of Coordination Problems

  • Unsteady walking pattern
  • Difficulty maintaining balance
  • Poor movement control
  • Frequent stumbling
  • Reduced mobility confidence
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Fear of falling
  • Reduced coordination during movement
