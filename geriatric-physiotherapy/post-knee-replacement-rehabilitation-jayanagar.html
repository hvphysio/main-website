Why Is Physiotherapy Important After Knee Replacement?

Physiotherapy after knee replacement surgery is essential to improve knee mobility, reduce stiffness, restore walking ability and regain strength. Proper rehabilitation helps patients return to daily activities safely and improves long-term functional recovery.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced post knee replacement rehabilitation focused on pain management, strengthening, balance training and gait rehabilitation.

Common Problems After Knee Replacement Surgery

  • Knee pain and stiffness
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced knee mobility
  • Weakness in leg muscles
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Swelling around the knee
  • Reduced confidence during walking
