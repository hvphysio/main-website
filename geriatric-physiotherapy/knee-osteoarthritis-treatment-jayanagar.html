What Is Knee Osteoarthritis?

Knee osteoarthritis is a degenerative joint condition commonly affecting elderly individuals. It occurs due to gradual wear and tear of knee joint cartilage, leading to pain, stiffness, reduced mobility and difficulty walking or climbing stairs.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide advanced geriatric physiotherapy focused on reducing knee pain, improving mobility, strengthening muscles and enhancing walking ability.

Common Symptoms of Knee Osteoarthritis

  • Knee pain during walking
  • Joint stiffness
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Reduced walking tolerance
  • Swelling around the knee
  • Cracking or grinding sensation
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Reduced balance and mobility
