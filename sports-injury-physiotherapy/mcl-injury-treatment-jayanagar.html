What Is an MCL Injury?

An MCL injury affects the medial collateral ligament located on the inner side of the knee. It commonly occurs during sports because of sudden twisting, direct impact or awkward knee movement. MCL injuries may cause knee pain, swelling and instability during movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured MCL rehabilitation focused on pain relief, knee stability, strengthening, balance improvement and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of MCL Injury

  • Pain on the inner side of the knee
  • Swelling around the knee joint
  • Difficulty walking or bending the knee
  • Feeling of knee instability
  • Pain during sports activities
  • Weakness in leg muscles
  • Reduced balance and movement confidence
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
