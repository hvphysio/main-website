What Is an Ankle Sprain?

An ankle sprain occurs when the ligaments supporting the ankle are stretched or injured because of twisting, sudden direction changes, jumping or sports activities. It commonly affects athletes, runners and active individuals.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured ankle rehabilitation focused on pain relief, stability improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Ankle Sprain

  • Ankle pain during walking or movement
  • Swelling around the ankle joint
  • Difficulty bearing weight on the foot
  • Reduced ankle stability
  • Pain during sports or running activities
  • Balance and coordination problems
  • Stiffness and tenderness around the ankle
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
