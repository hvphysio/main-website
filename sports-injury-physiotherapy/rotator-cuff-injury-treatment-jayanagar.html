What Is a Rotator Cuff Injury?

A rotator cuff injury affects the muscles and tendons that stabilize the shoulder joint. It commonly occurs during sports involving repetitive overhead movement, throwing, lifting or sudden shoulder strain. Rotator cuff injuries may cause pain, weakness and reduced shoulder mobility.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured shoulder rehabilitation focused on pain relief, mobility improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Rotator Cuff Injury

  • Shoulder pain during movement
  • Pain while lifting the arm
  • Weakness in the shoulder muscles
  • Difficulty performing overhead activities
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Pain during sports activities
  • Muscle tightness and stiffness
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
