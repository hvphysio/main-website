What Is Shoulder Impingement?

Shoulder impingement occurs when the tendons or soft tissues of the shoulder become compressed during arm movement. It commonly affects athletes and individuals performing repetitive overhead activities such as throwing, lifting or gym training.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured shoulder rehabilitation focused on pain relief, mobility improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Shoulder Impingement

  • Shoulder pain during arm movement
  • Pain while lifting the arm overhead
  • Reduced shoulder mobility
  • Weakness in shoulder muscles
  • Pain during sports or gym activities
  • Difficulty sleeping on the affected side
  • Shoulder stiffness and tightness
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
