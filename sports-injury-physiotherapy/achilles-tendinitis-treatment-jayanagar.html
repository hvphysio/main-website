What Is Achilles Tendinitis?

Achilles Tendinitis is an overuse injury affecting the Achilles tendon located at the back of the ankle. It commonly occurs in runners, athletes and physically active individuals because of repetitive stress, sudden increase in activity or improper movement mechanics.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on pain relief, tendon healing, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Achilles Tendinitis

  • Pain at the back of the heel or ankle
  • Morning stiffness in the tendon
  • Pain during running or jumping
  • Tenderness along the Achilles tendon
  • Difficulty pushing off the foot
  • Calf muscle tightness
  • Reduced sports performance
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
