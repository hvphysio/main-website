What Is a Muscle Strain?

A muscle strain occurs when muscle fibers are overstretched or injured during sports, exercise or sudden physical activity. It commonly affects athletes and active individuals and may lead to pain, swelling, tightness and reduced movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on pain relief, flexibility improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Muscle Strain

  • Muscle pain during movement
  • Muscle tightness or stiffness
  • Swelling or tenderness
  • Difficulty stretching the muscle
  • Reduced strength and flexibility
  • Pain during sports activities
  • Difficulty running or exercising
  • Reduced sports performance
