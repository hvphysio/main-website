What Is Golfer’s Elbow?

Golfer’s Elbow, also known as medial epicondylitis, is a condition caused by overuse or strain of the forearm muscles and tendons attached to the inner side of the elbow. It commonly affects golfers, gym users, racquet sport players and individuals performing repetitive gripping activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on pain relief, tendon recovery, grip strengthening and safe return to sports and daily activities.

Common Symptoms of Golfer’s Elbow

  • Pain on the inner side of the elbow
  • Pain during gripping or lifting
  • Weak grip strength
  • Difficulty holding objects
  • Pain during sports or gym activities
  • Forearm muscle tightness
  • Reduced arm strength and function
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
