What Is Post Injury Rehabilitation?

Post injury rehabilitation focuses on restoring strength, flexibility, balance, mobility and functional performance after sports injuries, muscle injuries, ligament injuries or joint injuries. Proper rehabilitation helps athletes and active individuals safely return to activity and reduce the risk of re-injury.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation programs tailored to individual recovery goals and sports requirements.

Common Problems After Sports Injuries

  • Muscle weakness after injury
  • Joint stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Pain during movement or sports activity
  • Reduced balance and coordination
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
  • Loss of flexibility and endurance
  • Fear of re-injury during activity
  • Reduced sports performance
