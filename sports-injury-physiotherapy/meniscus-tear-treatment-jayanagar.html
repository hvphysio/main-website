What Is a Meniscus Tear?

A meniscus tear is a common knee injury involving damage to the cartilage that cushions the knee joint. It can occur during sports because of twisting movements, sudden direction changes or deep squatting. Meniscus tears may cause pain, swelling and restricted knee movement.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured meniscus rehabilitation focused on pain relief, knee mobility, strengthening, balance training and safe return to activity.

Common Symptoms of Meniscus Tear

  • Knee pain during walking or bending
  • Swelling around the knee joint
  • Difficulty squatting or climbing stairs
  • Knee locking or catching sensation
  • Reduced knee mobility
  • Weakness in leg muscles
  • Feeling of instability during movement
  • Difficulty returning to sports activities
