What Is a Foot Stress Injury?

Foot stress injuries develop because of repetitive loading, overuse, prolonged running, jumping or sports activities that place excessive stress on the foot structures. They commonly affect runners, athletes and physically active individuals.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on pain relief, mobility improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports and daily activities.

Common Symptoms of Foot Stress Injury

  • Foot pain during walking or running
  • Pain after sports activities
  • Difficulty bearing weight on the foot
  • Swelling or tenderness in the foot
  • Reduced walking or running comfort
  • Foot stiffness and tightness
  • Reduced sports performance
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
