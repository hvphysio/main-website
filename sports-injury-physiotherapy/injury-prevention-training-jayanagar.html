What Is Injury Prevention Training?

Injury prevention training focuses on improving strength, flexibility, balance, coordination and movement mechanics to reduce the risk of sports injuries and overuse problems. It is beneficial for athletes, runners, gym users and physically active individuals.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide personalized injury prevention programs designed to improve sports performance and reduce the chances of future injuries.
