What Is a Stress Fracture?

A stress fracture is a small bone injury caused by repetitive loading and overuse during running, jumping or high impact sports activities. It commonly affects runners, athletes and physically active individuals involved in repetitive training.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on safe recovery, strengthening, movement correction and gradual return to sports activity after medical clearance.

Common Symptoms of Stress Fracture

  • Localized pain during activity
  • Pain increasing with running or jumping
  • Difficulty walking comfortably
  • Tenderness over the affected bone
  • Swelling around the injured area
  • Reduced sports performance
  • Pain during weight-bearing activities
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
