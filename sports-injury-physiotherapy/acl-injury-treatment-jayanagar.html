What Is an ACL Injury?

An ACL injury affects the anterior cruciate ligament of the knee and commonly occurs during sports involving sudden twisting, jumping, landing or direction change. It may cause knee pain, swelling, instability and difficulty returning to sports safely.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured ACL rehabilitation focused on knee stability, strengthening, balance, movement correction and safe return to activity.

Common Symptoms of ACL Injury

  • Knee pain and swelling after injury
  • Feeling of knee giving way
  • Difficulty walking or running
  • Reduced knee stability
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Weakness in thigh and leg muscles
  • Reduced balance and confidence
  • Difficulty returning to sports activities
