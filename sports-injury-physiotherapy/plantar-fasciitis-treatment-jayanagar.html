What Is Plantar Fasciitis?

Plantar Fasciitis is a common condition caused by irritation or overuse of the plantar fascia, a thick band of tissue supporting the arch of the foot. It commonly affects runners, athletes and individuals who stand or walk for long durations.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on heel pain relief, flexibility improvement, strengthening and safe return to sports and daily activities.

Common Symptoms of Plantar Fasciitis

  • Sharp heel pain during first steps in the morning
  • Pain after prolonged standing or walking
  • Tightness in the foot or calf muscles
  • Pain during running or sports activities
  • Tenderness under the heel
  • Reduced walking comfort
  • Difficulty performing sports activities
  • Reduced mobility and performance
