What Is a Muscle Tear?

A muscle tear occurs when muscle fibers are partially or completely damaged because of sudden force, overstretching or intense sports activity. Muscle tears commonly affect athletes and active individuals and may cause severe pain, swelling and weakness.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation focused on pain relief, tissue healing, strengthening and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Muscle Tear

  • Sudden sharp muscle pain
  • Swelling and tenderness
  • Difficulty moving the affected muscle
  • Weakness during activity
  • Muscle tightness and stiffness
  • Pain during sports movements
  • Reduced flexibility and strength
  • Difficulty returning to sports activities
