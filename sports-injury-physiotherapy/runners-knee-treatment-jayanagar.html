What Is Runner’s Knee?

Runner’s Knee, also known as patellofemoral pain syndrome, is a common sports injury causing pain around the kneecap. It often develops because of overuse, muscle imbalance, improper running mechanics or repetitive stress during sports activities.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured rehabilitation programs focused on pain relief, movement correction, strengthening, flexibility and safe return to activity.

Common Symptoms of Runner’s Knee

  • Pain around or behind the kneecap
  • Pain during running or squatting
  • Difficulty climbing stairs
  • Knee discomfort after prolonged sitting
  • Weakness in thigh and hip muscles
  • Tightness in surrounding muscles
  • Reduced sports performance
  • Difficulty returning to running activities
