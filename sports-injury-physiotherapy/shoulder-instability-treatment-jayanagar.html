What Is Shoulder Instability?

Shoulder instability occurs when the shoulder joint becomes loose or repeatedly slips out of position because of injury, dislocation or weakness in the supporting muscles and ligaments. It commonly affects athletes involved in throwing, swimming, gym training and contact sports.

At Health Vision Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation in Jayanagar Bangalore, we provide structured shoulder rehabilitation focused on stability improvement, strengthening, movement control and safe return to sports activity.

Common Symptoms of Shoulder Instability

  • Feeling of shoulder slipping or shifting
  • Shoulder pain during movement
  • Weakness in shoulder muscles
  • Reduced shoulder stability
  • Difficulty lifting or throwing
  • Fear during overhead activities
  • Repeated shoulder dislocation episodes
  • Difficulty returning to sports safely
